Good to know

Understand your conversations

WhatsApp's export is a plain text file with one line per message. This reader parses those lines, joins multi-line messages and handles both 12-hour and 24-hour exports, then turns them into charts you can scan in seconds.

Because chats are personal, nothing is uploaded. Close the tab and the data is gone. Date formats differ by region, so weekday charts are best-effort when a chat's day and month order is ambiguous.
