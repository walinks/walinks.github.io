Tips before you join

🔎

Check the source

Treat a group post as a pointer. Open the official advertisement or department site before you apply.

🚫

Never pay to apply

Genuine government posts do not ask for fees through chat. Be wary of anyone who asks for money or a CNIC photo.

🔔

Mute and check daily

Busy groups get hundreds of messages. Mute notifications and scan once or twice a day for closing dates.
