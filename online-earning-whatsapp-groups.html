Tips before you join

Be sceptical of guarantees

If a post promises fixed daily income for little work, it is almost always a scam. Real freelance income depends on skills and clients.

🚫

Never send money first

Do not pay registration, training or security fees to an unknown person to start a task.

🔔

Build a portfolio

Use leads from groups to apply on established platforms, and keep samples of your work ready.
