Good to know

How WhatsApp formatting works

WhatsApp turns simple markers into formatting: asterisks make bold, underscores italic, tildes strikethrough and triple backticks monospace. This tool adds those markers around your selection so you don't have to remember them.

Fancy fonts are different: they swap letters for look-alike Unicode characters, so they can be pasted anywhere. They are decorative, so use them sparingly and avoid them for important information.
