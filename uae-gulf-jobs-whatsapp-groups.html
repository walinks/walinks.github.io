Good to know

Tips before you join

🔎

Verify the employer

Look up the company and its licence before sharing documents. Real employers do not charge recruitment fees to candidates.

🚫

Keep documents private

Share passport or visa copies only with an employer you have verified, never in a public group.

🔔

Match the role

Join one or two groups for your trade rather than many, so important posts do not get buried.
